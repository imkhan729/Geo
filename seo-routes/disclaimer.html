GPS coordinates are editable and not proof of location

EXIF GPS metadata is plain text inside an image file. It can be added, changed, or removed by anyone — including with our own tools. A geotag is not proof that a photo was taken at a particular place or time, and geotagged photos should not be relied on as legal evidence of attendance, delivery, or presence at a location.

Similarly, removing GPS data with our Remove GPS tool deletes the coordinates stored in the file, but copies of the photo shared earlier, thumbnails, or sidecar files may still carry location information elsewhere.
