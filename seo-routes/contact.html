Contact FreeGeoTagger

By FreeGeoTagger · Updated September 26, 2026

For tool problems, suggestions or corrections to a guide, email contact@freegeotagger.com. There is no account or support form to complete. Response times are not guaranteed.

Report a reproducible problem

Include the page URL, browser and operating system, image format and approximate file size. Describe what you clicked, what you expected, and the exact error or result. For batch imports, describe the column headings and whether filenames match; remove private coordinates before sharing examples.

Do not attach personal photographs, camera serial numbers, home addresses or location histories. Start with a written description. If a sample is necessary, use a non-sensitive image you have permission to share. Sending an email is separate from the tools' local image processing.

Try these checks first

  • No GPS found: inspect the original camera file with EXIF Viewer. A screenshot or a copy from messaging may not retain metadata. Missing coordinates cannot be recovered by reading EXIF.
  • Export fails: try one image under 20 MB, keep the tab open, and leave enough device memory. Keep the original when testing another format.
  • Map or address search fails: check the connection. You can enter known latitude and longitude manually; third-party map availability is separate from image processing.
  • The location looks wrong: check latitude/longitude order and the minus sign for south or west. Reopen the downloaded copy, not the original.

Report an editorial correction

Include the article URL, the affected sentence and an official reference or reproducible example. Product interfaces change, so include your operating system or app version when relevant. Our About page explains our editorial approach and tool limitations.

Privacy requests

Use the same email address for privacy questions. The browser tools do not retain your photos on our servers, so we cannot retrieve a lost upload or download. For information about analytics, map requests and browser preferences, read our privacy policy and cookie policy.
