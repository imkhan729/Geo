## Notable rewrites and why

**`/` — 76 → 52 chars.** The old title was truncated in SERPs around *"…to Any Photo Onl…"*, hiding the brand entirely on the site's highest-impression page. The new title fits in full.

**`/blog/best-free-photo-geotagging-tools` — added a number.** Now "6 Best Free Photo Geotagging Tools in 2026 Compared". The article genuinely reviews six tools (FreeGeoTagger, GeoSetter, HoudahGeo, Lightroom, digiKam, ExifTool), so the count is truthful, not inflated for CTR. This page defends `free geotagging tool`, which is down 50% in GSC.

**`/gps-finder` — reframed to searcher phrasing.** "GPS Photo Finder – See Where Any Photo Was Taken Free" matches how people actually phrase the query ("where was this photo taken") rather than the tool-centric "Extract GPS Coordinates".

**Legal pages — 29–32 → 45–53 chars.** "Cookie Policy | FreeGeoTagger" wasted the whole title on brand. The new titles describe what the page answers, which is what earns a click.

**Brand suffix dropped where it did not fit.** "| FreeGeoTagger" costs 16 characters. On a 60-character budget it was crowding out the keyword, so it is now used only where it fits. Brand queries (`geotagger`, +95%) already rank on brand recognition; the titles now compete for intent queries instead.

**Runtime suffix bug fixed.** `updatePageSEO()` used to append " | GeoTagger" to any title lacking that string, which would have pushed every newly tuned title back over the limit at runtime. Removed.
